PROJECT 01 · MODERN KITCHEN

Modern Kitchen — Designed for Function & Precision.

A complete kitchen design developed around functionality, clean aesthetics and practical execution. The project combines 3D visualization with technical planning and detailed coordination.

Project Type Kitchen Design
Scope Design · 3D · Technical
Deliverables Visualization & Drawings

Project Overview

The kitchen was designed to maximize storage, maintain a clean modern appearance and provide a practical workflow between the main working zones. Appliance locations, cabinet dimensions and technical requirements were considered during the design process.

Technical Drawings

The design is supported by technical drawings that communicate dimensions and execution details required for manufacturing and installation.
